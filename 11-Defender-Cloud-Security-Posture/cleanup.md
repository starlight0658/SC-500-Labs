# Cleanup — Lab 11

Most of this lab is read-only review of Defender for Cloud recommendations. Cleanup depends on the underlying resources.

- Stop/deallocate lab VMs when they are not needed.
- Disable paid Defender plans that were enabled only for temporary lab testing if they are no longer required.
- Remove temporary Foundry/AI resources after their evidence is captured if they are still incurring cost.
- Keep screenshots and documentation, but do not retain unnecessary cloud resources solely for portfolio evidence.
