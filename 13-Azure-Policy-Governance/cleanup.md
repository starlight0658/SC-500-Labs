# Cleanup — Lab 13

Azure Policy itself has low direct cost, but policy-created or monitored resources may incur cost.

- Keep reusable policy definitions if they are useful for future labs.
- Remove temporary assignments/remediation tasks only if they could interfere with later labs.
- Leave secure settings such as anonymous blob access disabled unless a later lab explicitly requires another configuration.
- Remove temporary test resources rather than weakening the governance control to reduce cost.
